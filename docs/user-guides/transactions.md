# Transactions — Sales, Purchases & Expenses

Transactions are the heart of NairaFlow. Every time money moves in or out of your business, you record a transaction. This keeps your books accurate and your reports meaningful.

There are three types of transactions: **Sales**, **Purchases**, and **Expenses**.

---

## Recording a Sale

Use this when a customer buys from you — whether it's a quick counter sale or a larger order.

### What You Enter

- **Products sold** — Which items, how many, and at what price.
- **Payment method** — How the customer paid:
  - **Cash** — Physical cash received.
  - **Transfer** — Bank transfer to your account.
  - **POS** — Card payment via POS terminal.
  - **Credit** — The customer will pay later (creates a debt record automatically).
- **Customer** (optional) — If this is a regular customer, link them from your contacts. For walk-in customers, you can leave this blank or just enter a name.

### What Happens Automatically

1. **Stock is updated** — The quantity of each product sold is deducted from your inventory instantly.
2. **A debt is created** (if credit) — If you select "credit" as the payment method, a receivable debt is automatically created so you can track what the customer owes.
3. **Profit is tracked** — The system records both the selling price and cost price for each item, enabling accurate profit reporting later.

### Service Sales (No Inventory)

You can also record sales for services or items that aren't in your inventory. Instead of selecting a product, just type a description (e.g., "Delivery fee" or "Consultation") along with the quantity and price. Stock won't be affected since there's no physical product involved.

---

## Recording a Purchase

Use this when you buy stock or supplies from a supplier.

### What You Enter

- **Products bought** — Which items, how many, and at what cost.
- **Payment method** — Same options as sales (cash, transfer, POS, or credit).
- **Supplier** (optional) — Link to a contact if it's a regular supplier.
- **Description** (optional) — A note like "Weekly restock" for your records.

### What Happens Automatically

1. **Stock is updated** — The quantity of each product is added to your inventory.
2. **A debt is created** (if credit) — If you buy on credit, a payable debt is created so you can track what you owe the supplier.

---

## Recording an Expense

Use this for costs that aren't purchases of stock — things like rent, salaries, electricity, internet, transport, or maintenance.

### What You Enter

- **Amount** — How much was spent.
- **Category** — What type of expense (e.g., Rent, Salary, Utilities, Transport).
- **Description** (optional) — A note like "Shop rent for May" or "Staff salary — Musa."

Expenses don't affect inventory, but they do appear in your cash book and reports, helping you understand your true business costs.

---

## Viewing Your Transaction History

You can browse all your past transactions, with options to:

- **Filter by type** — See only sales, only purchases, or only expenses.
- **Filter by date** — Focus on a specific time period.
- **Filter by contact** — See all transactions with a particular customer or supplier.
- **Drill into details** — Tap any transaction to see the full breakdown, including which products were involved, quantities, and prices.

---

## Quick Summary

| Transaction Type | When to Use | Affects Stock? | Creates Debt? |
|-----------------|-------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Sale** | Customer buys from you | Decreases stock | If on credit |
| **Purchase** | You buy stock from a supplier | Increases stock | If on credit |
| **Expense** | Business costs (rent, salary, etc.) | No | No |
