# Your Business Profile

Your business profile is the central identity of your shop or company in NairaFlow. Everything — your products, transactions, invoices, and reports — is tied to this profile.

---

## What's in Your Profile?

When you first registered, you provided your business name. You can add more details later to make your invoices and receipts look professional:

- **Business Name** — How your business appears on invoices, receipts, and the app dashboard.
- **Email Address** — Optional, for receiving digital receipts or communications.
- **Business Category** — What type of business you run (e.g., Retail, Food & Drinks, Services, Wholesale). This helps the app tailor your experience.
- **State and City** — Where your business is located.
- **Business Address** — Your physical shop or office address, printed on invoices.
- **Bank Details** — Your bank name and account number. These appear on formal invoices so customers know where to pay you.

---

## Adding Your Bank Details

NairaFlow includes a bank account validator. When you enter your account number and select your bank, the system can verify the account name matches. This helps avoid errors when printing bank details on invoices.

---

## Daily Bookkeeping Reminders

One of NairaFlow's most helpful features is the **daily reminder**. You can set a time, and the app will send you a notification every day reminding you to record your sales and expenses for the day.

For example, if you set your reminder to **8:30 PM**, NairaFlow will ping you at that time each evening: *"Don't forget to record your sales for today!"*

This helps you build the habit of closing your books daily — no more forgetting to log transactions and losing track of your money.

### How to Set It Up

1. Go to your Business Profile or Settings.
2. Set your preferred **Reminder Time** (in 24-hour format, e.g., `20:30` for 8:30 PM).
3. Make sure you allow notifications from the NairaFlow app on your phone.

You can change this time anytime. If you don't want reminders, just turn them off.

---

## Who Can Edit the Profile?

Only the **business owner** (the person who created the account) can change the business profile. Staff members cannot edit these details — this protects your bank information and business settings from being changed by employees.
