# Reports & Dashboard

The Reports section turns your day-to-day transactions into meaningful insights about your business's financial health. The Dashboard gives you a quick snapshot every time you open the app.

> **Important:** Reports are only visible to the **business owner**. Staff members cannot access financial reports. This keeps your sensitive business numbers private.

---

## The Dashboard — Your Home Screen

The dashboard is designed to give you an instant overview. It combines several key numbers into one screen:

### Today's Summary
- **Money In** — Total cash, transfer, and POS payments received today from sales and debt repayments.
- **Money Out** — Total spent today on purchases, expenses, and debt payments.
- **Net Cash Flow** — Money In minus Money Out. A positive number means you made more than you spent today. A negative number means you spent more than you made.

### Debt Overview
- **Total Receivable** — All money customers still owe your business.
- **Total Payable** — All money your business still owes suppliers.
- **Net Debt** — Receivables minus payables. A positive number is healthy; a negative number means you owe more than you're owed.

### Low Stock Alerts
A simple count of products that are at or below their low-stock threshold. Tap to see which products need restocking.

### Recent Transactions
The last 5 transactions recorded, so you can quickly review recent activity.

## Cash Book — Your Full Transaction Log

The Cash Book is a complete, chronological record of every financial movement in your business. Think of it as your digital ledger.

Each entry shows:
- **Type** — Sale, Purchase, Expense, or Debt Repayment.
- **Amount** — How much money moved.
- **Payment Method** — Cash, transfer, or POS.
- **Contact** — Who it was with (if linked to a contact).
- **Date and Time** — When it happened.

You can filter the Cash Book by transaction type to focus on specific activity — for example, viewing only expenses to review your costs.

---

## Period Summary — Pick a Date Range

Want to know how your business performed last week? Last month? Over the festive season?

The Period Summary lets you pick a **start date** and **end date**, and it calculates:

- **Money In** within that period.
- **Money Out** within that period.
- **Net Cash Flow** (in minus out).
- **Total number of transactions**.

This is great for:
- Comparing one month to another.
- Preparing informal financial reviews.
- Understanding seasonal trends in your business.

---

## All-Time Totals

Curious about your overall numbers since you started using NairaFlow? The All-Time view shows:

- **Total Sales** — Lifetime revenue from sales.
- **Total Purchases** — Lifetime spending on stock.
- **Total Repayments** — Lifetime debt repayments received.

Use this for a high-level sense of your business's scale and growth.
