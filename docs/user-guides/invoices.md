# Invoices

Invoices are formal documents you issue to customers — typically for larger orders, services, or when payment isn't made upfront. They're professional, numbered, and trackable.

---

## When to Use an Invoice vs. a Sale

| Use a **Sale** (Transaction) | Use an **Invoice** |
|------------------------------|---------------------|
| Quick, over-the-counter sales | Large orders or services |
| Payment received immediately | Payment will come later |
| No formal document needed | Customer needs a professional bill |
| Walk-in customers | Business-to-business dealings |

---

## How Invoices Work

### Creating an Invoice

When you create an invoice, you provide:

1. **Customer name** — Who the invoice is for.
2. **Line items** — What you're billing for. Each line has:
   - A description (e.g., "Interior painting — 3 rooms")
   - Quantity (e.g., 1 job)
   - Unit price (e.g., ₦50,000 per room)

The system automatically calculates the total. It can also handle discounts, taxes, and shipping charges if you add them.

### Invoice Numbering

Every invoice gets a unique sequential number — `INV-001`, `INV-002`, `INV-003`, and so on. Numbers never repeat, and they're unique to your business. This keeps your records organized and professional.

---

## Invoice Statuses

An invoice moves through three stages based on how much has been paid:

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pending** | No payment has been made yet. |
| **Deposit** | Some payment has been made, but not the full amount. |
| **Paid** | The full invoice amount has been received. |

---

## Recording Payments on an Invoice

When a customer makes a payment:

1. Open the invoice.
2. Tap **Record Payment**.
3. Enter the amount and payment method (cash or transfer).

The system automatically:
- Updates the invoice status (Pending → Deposit → Paid).
- Creates a transaction in your cash book, so the money is reflected in your reports and daily summary.

This means you don't need to separately record a sale — the invoice payment handles it for you.

---

## Downloading as PDF

You can generate a PDF version of any invoice to share with customers via email, WhatsApp, or print. The PDF includes:

- Your business name and details (including bank information for payments).
- The invoice number and date.
- A breakdown of all line items.
- The total amount and outstanding balance.

This is especially useful for business clients who need formal documentation for their own records.

---

## Cancelling an Invoice

You can void (cancel) an invoice, but **only if no payments have been made on it yet**. This prevents you from accidentally cancelling an invoice that a customer has already partially paid. If a customer has paid and you need to reverse it, you'll need to handle that through your transaction records instead.

| Action | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
