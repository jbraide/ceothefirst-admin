# NairaFlow — User Guide

Welcome to NairaFlow! This guide explains everything you need to know to manage your business's books — in plain, simple language.

---

## What Is NairaFlow?

NairaFlow is a bookkeeping app built for small and medium-sized businesses in Nigeria. It helps you:

- **Track your sales and expenses** — Know exactly how much money comes in and goes out every day.
- **Manage your inventory** — Keep track of what products you have, what's selling, and what's running low.
- **Handle credit sales** — Never lose track of who owes you money.
- **Issue professional invoices** — Send formal bills to your business customers.
- **See your financial health** — Get clear reports on how your business is doing.
- **Let your staff help** — Give employees limited access so they can record sales without seeing your finances.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is my data safe?
Yes. Your PIN is encrypted and never stored in plain form. Your data is stored securely and only accessible by logging into your account.

### Can I use NairaFlow for multiple businesses?
Each phone number can only have one business account. If you run multiple businesses, you'll need separate phone numbers for each.

### What if I sell both products and services?
NairaFlow handles both. For products, they're tracked in your inventory with stock levels. For services, you can record sales with just a description — no stock tracking needed.

### What happens if my staff makes a mistake recording a sale?
Currently, the best approach is to record a correcting transaction or manual stock adjustment with a note explaining the correction. Always review your cash book regularly to catch errors early.

### Can I delete old transactions?
To protect the integrity of your financial records, transactions should not be deleted. If you need to correct something, speak with support about the best approach.

### Does NairaFlow work offline?
NairaFlow requires an internet connection to sync your data securely. Make sure you have a connection when recording transactions.

### What if a customer pays part of what they owe?
Record a partial payment against their debt. The debt status will remain "Open" until the full amount is paid, and the payment will appear in your cash book automatically.

### Can I export my data?
Currently, NairaFlow provides reports within the app. PDF invoice exports are available. Additional export options may be added in future updates.

## A Typical Day with NairaFlow

Here's how NairaFlow fits into your daily routine:

1. **Morning** — Open the dashboard to see yesterday's numbers and check for low-stock alerts.
2. **Throughout the day** — Record sales as they happen (or let your staff do it). Record any expenses or purchases.
3. **Evening** — Your daily reminder prompts you to make sure everything is recorded. Review today's transactions in the cash book.
4. **End of week/month** — Check your period summary to see how the business performed.
