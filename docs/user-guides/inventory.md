# Inventory — Products & Stock

The Inventory section is where you manage everything you sell. It covers your **products**, how they're organized into **categories**, and your current stock levels.

---

## Categories: Organizing Your Products

Categories help you group similar products together. This makes browsing, reporting, and managing your inventory much easier.

### Examples of Categories
- **Drinks** — Water, soft drinks, juice
- **Snacks** — Chips, biscuits, candy
- **Building Materials** — Cement, rods, sand
- **Groceries** — Rice, beans, oil

You can create as many categories as you need. Each category has a name and an optional icon for easy identification in the app.

### Deleting a Category
You can only delete a category if no products are assigned to it. If products are still linked, you'll need to move or delete those products first. This prevents you from accidentally losing track of your inventory.

---

## Products: What You Sell

Each product in your inventory has the following details:

| Detail | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Name** | What the product is called (e.g., "Coca-Cola 50cl") |
| **Category** | Which group it belongs to |
| **Selling Price** | How much you sell it for |
| **Cost Price** | How much it costs you to buy/acquire |
| **Stock Level** | How many units you currently have |
| **Low Stock Threshold** | When to alert you that stock is running low |
| **Image** | A photo of the product (optional) |

### Why Cost Price Matters
Entering your cost price lets NairaFlow calculate your profit margins. When you sell a product, the system knows the difference between what you sold it for and what it cost you. This feeds into your financial reports so you can see which products are most profitable.

### Stock Level
Stock is automatically updated when you:
- **Record a sale** → Stock goes down.
- **Record a purchase** → Stock goes up.
- **Manually adjust stock** → For situations like damaged goods, theft, or corrections.

---

## Low Stock Alerts

Every product has a **low stock threshold** that you set. When the stock level drops to or below that number, the product is flagged as **low stock**.

For example, if you sell bottled water and set the threshold to 5, the app will alert you when you have 5 bottles or fewer left — giving you time to restock before running out.

The app's dashboard also shows a count of how many products are currently low on stock, so you can see at a glance if you need to reorder.

---

## Manual Stock Adjustments

Sometimes stock levels change for reasons other than sales or purchases — perhaps items were damaged, stolen, or you found a counting error. You can manually adjust stock with a note explaining why:

- **Positive adjustment** — Add stock (e.g., found extra items during a count).
- **Negative adjustment** — Remove stock (e.g., damaged goods, theft).

Every adjustment is recorded with a reason and an optional note, so there's always an audit trail.

---

## Searching and Filtering

- **Search by name** — Type part of a product name to find it quickly.
- **Filter by category** — View only products in a specific category.

---

## Quick Summary

| Action | What Happens |
|--------|-------------|
| Create a category | Organize products into groups |
| Add a product | Start tracking a new item with price and stock |
| Record a sale | Stock decreases automatically |
| Record a purchase | Stock increases automatically |
| Adjust stock manually | Correct stock for damage, theft, or errors |
| Low stock alert | App notifies you when stock is below your set threshold |
