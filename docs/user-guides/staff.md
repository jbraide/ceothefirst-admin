# Staff Management

The Staff feature lets you give your employees access to NairaFlow — but with clear limits. They can do their jobs (like recording sales and checking stock) without seeing sensitive business information.

---

## Why Add Staff?

If you have employees who help run your shop, giving them their own NairaFlow access means:

- They can **record sales** directly in the app instead of writing them down for you to enter later.
- They can **check stock levels** and see what's available.
- You can **track who recorded what** — every transaction is linked to the staff member who created it.
- You **stay in control** — they can't see your financial reports or change business settings.

---

## Adding a Staff Member

As the business owner, you can add staff members from the Staff section. You'll need to provide:

1. **Name** — The employee's full name.
2. **Phone number** — Their unique phone number (they'll use this to log in).
3. **A 4-digit PIN** — You set an initial PIN for them. They should change it after logging in.

---

## What Staff Can Do

Staff members have a focused set of permissions:

| Allowed | Not Allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Record sales and purchases | View financial reports |
| Check product stock levels | See cost prices (your profit margins are hidden) |
| View and manage inventory | Edit business profile or bank details |
| See customer/supplier contacts | Add or manage other staff members |
| Log in with their own PIN | Access debt summaries or all-time totals |

This gives your team the tools they need for daily operations while protecting your business's financial privacy.

---

## What Staff Cannot See

Two specific things are hidden from staff:

1. **Cost Prices** — When a staff member views a product or transaction, the cost price (what you paid for the item) is hidden. They only see selling prices. This keeps your profit margins confidential.
2. **Financial Reports** — The Reports section is entirely off-limits to staff. They cannot see how much money the business is making, your cash book, or your debt position.

---

## Deactivating a Staff Member

If an employee leaves or you want to revoke their access:

- You can **deactivate** their account. This preserves their transaction history (so you still know who recorded past sales) but prevents them from logging in.
- You can also **reset their PIN** if they've forgotten it.

---

## Staff Login

Staff members log in the same way you do — with their phone number and PIN — but they use a dedicated staff login option. The system recognizes them as staff and automatically applies the right restrictions.

---

## Quick Summary

| Action | Who Can Do It |
|--------|--------------|
| Add staff | Owner only |
| Deactivate staff | Owner only |
| Reset staff PIN | Owner only |
| Record sales | Owner and staff |
| View stock | Owner and staff |
| See cost prices | Owner only |
| View reports | Owner only |
| Edit business profile | Owner only |
