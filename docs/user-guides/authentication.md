# Getting Started & Authentication

Getting into NairaFlow is simple. You only need your **phone number** and a **4-digit PIN** — no email, no passwords, no complicated signup forms.

---

## Creating Your Account

When you open NairaFlow for the first time, you'll be asked for three things:

1. **Your phone number** — This is your unique identifier. Each phone number can only have one NairaFlow account.
2. **A 4-digit PIN** — This is your secret code for logging in. Choose something you'll remember but others can't easily guess. Avoid obvious numbers like `0000` or `1234`.
3. **Your business name** — The name of your shop or business as you want it to appear on receipts and invoices.

That's it. Once you register, you're in.

---

## Logging In

Every time you open the app, you enter your phone number and your 4-digit PIN. The app then keeps you signed in for a while so you don't have to re-enter it every time.

If you close the app and come back later, you may be asked to log in again. This is a security measure to protect your business data.

---

## What If I Forget My PIN?

No problem. There's a "Forgot PIN" option on the login screen:

1. Tap **Forgot PIN** and enter your phone number.
2. You'll receive a **one-time code (OTP)** via SMS to that phone number.
3. Enter the code you received, then choose a new 4-digit PIN.
4. You can now log in with your new PIN.

The OTP expires after 5 minutes, so use it quickly. If it expires, just request a new one.

---

## Security: How Your PIN Is Protected

- NairaFlow never stores your actual PIN. It's scrambled using industry-standard encryption before being saved. Even if someone gained access to the database, they couldn't see your real PIN.
- Your login session is secured with tokens that automatically refresh in the background. If someone somehow got hold of your old login token, it becomes useless as soon as your app refreshes it.

---

## Staff Login

If you're a staff member (an employee of the business), you have your own separate login. You'll use the same phone number and PIN format, but your access is limited — you can record sales and manage inventory, but you can't see financial reports or change business settings. More on that in the [Staff guide](./staff.md).

---

## Quick Summary

| Action | What You Need |
|--------|--------------|
| Sign up | Phone number, 4-digit PIN, business name |
| Log in | Phone number, 4-digit PIN |
| Reset PIN | Phone number, OTP sent via SMS, new PIN |

> **Tip:** Keep your PIN private. Don't share it with anyone — even staff members have their own separate accounts.
