# Debts — Receivables & Payables

The Debts section tracks money that is yet to change hands. Think of it as your "credit book" — it shows who owes you money (**receivables**) and who you owe money to (**payables**).

---

## How Debts Are Created

You don't create debts manually. They are generated automatically when you record certain transactions:

- **A customer buys on credit** → A **receivable** debt is created (money you'll collect later).
- **You buy stock on credit** → A **payable** debt is created (money you'll pay later).

This means every credit transaction leaves a clear, traceable record.

---

## Receivables (Money Owed to You)

These are debts your **customers** owe your business. You'll see each one with:

- **Who owes you** — The contact (customer) name.
- **Total amount** — The full value of the credit sale.
- **Amount paid so far** — How much the customer has already repaid.
- **Status** — **Open** (still owing) or **Closed** (fully paid).

### Example
You sell goods worth ₦15,000 to Mrs. Adeola on credit. A receivable of ₦15,000 is created. She later pays ₦10,000. The debt now shows: Total: ₦15,000, Paid: ₦10,000, Status: Open (₦5,000 remaining).

---

## Payables (Money You Owe)

These are debts your business owes to **suppliers**. They work the same way:

- **Who you owe** — The contact (supplier) name.
- **Total amount** — The full value of the credit purchase.
- **Amount you've paid** — How much you've already settled.
- **Status** — **Open** (still owing) or **Closed** (fully paid).

### Example
You buy ₦50,000 worth of stock from a distributor on credit. A payable of ₦50,000 is created. You pay ₦30,000. The debt now shows: Total: ₦50,000, Paid: ₦30,000, Status: Open (₦20,000 remaining).

---

## Recording a Repayment

When a customer pays part or all of what they owe, or when you pay a supplier:

1. Go to the debt record.
2. Tap **Record Payment**.
3. Enter the amount paid.

The system automatically:
- Adds the amount to the "paid" total.
- Changes the status to **Closed** if the full amount is now paid.
- Creates a transaction in your cash book, so your reports reflect the payment.

---

## Debt Summary

The Debt Summary gives you a bird's-eye view:

- **Total Receivables** — All money customers still owe you.
- **Total Payables** — All money you still owe suppliers.
- **Net Position** — Receivables minus payables. A positive number means you're owed more than you owe (good position). A negative number means you owe more than you're owed (watch your cash flow).

---

## Filtering Your Debts

You can filter your debt list by:

- **Type** — View only receivables or only payables.
- **Status** — View only open debts or only closed ones.
- **Contact** — View debts for a specific customer or supplier.

This helps you focus — for example, quickly seeing all open receivables when you need to follow up on unpaid customer balances.

---

## Quick Summary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Receivable | Money a customer owes your business |
| Payable | Money your business owes a supplier |
| Open | The debt is not yet fully paid |
| Closed | The debt has been fully settled |
| Net Position | Total owed to you minus total you owe |
